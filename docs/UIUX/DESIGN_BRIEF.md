# Gotabicho AI Redesign Brief: Modern Travel

This document serves as the primary source of truth for redesigning the Gotabicho app using AI tools (v0, Midjourney,
Claude Artifacts).

## 1. Core Identity & Aesthetic

- **Concept:** A modern digital "Travel Journal" (御旅帳 - Goshuin-cho style) that simplifies trip planning and expense
  tracking.
- **Aesthetic:** **Modern Travel / Minimalist Luxury**.
- **Key Visual Elements:**
    - **Typography:** Replace traditional "Hina" calligraphy with clean, geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter, SF Pro, or
      Montserrat) for a modern look.
    - **Styling:** Glassmorphism, soft shadows, large border-radii (20px+), and generous whitespace.
    - **Imagery:** High-quality travel photography as background elements or card covers.
- **Target Audience:** Modern travelers who value aesthetic organization and seamless AI-powered utilities.

## 2. Shared Feature Set (General)

- **Trip Dashboard:** A visual overview of current and upcoming trips with "Spent" progress indicators.
- **Digital Receipt Vault:** AI-powered extraction of costs and items from uploaded receipt photos.
- **Visual Bookmark Board:** A place to save "Inspiration" from social media (Instagram/Threads) with map-based
  discovery.
- **Budget Tracking:** Real-time visibility into group or individual spending.
- **Social Integration:** Shared trip IDs and participant management.

## 3. User Experience (UX) Goals

- **Minimize Friction:** "Quick-Add" actions for receipts and bookmarks should take < 3 seconds.
- **Contextual Intelligence:** The app should surface relevant bookmarks (spots) based on the user's current trip
  location.
- **Emotional Connection:** The design should feel like a "Personal Travel Diary" rather than just a utility.

## 4. Logo/Icon Redesign Specification

- **Symbolism:** An abstract "Travel Path" or "Continuous Line" that forms a subtle 'G' or a notebook shape.
- **Style:** Minimalist line art or flat vector.
- **Keywords:** Motion, Journal, Discovery, Connection.
